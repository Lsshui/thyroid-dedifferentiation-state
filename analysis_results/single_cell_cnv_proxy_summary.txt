Single-cell lightweight CNV proxy analysis

Method guardrail:
This is not inferCNV/copyKAT. It orders genes by TCGA/GENCODE genomic coordinates, centers log-normalized expression on non-epithelial reference cells, smooths across 100-gene genomic windows, and reports per-cell absolute deviation amplitude.
Use it as a reproducible proxy to test whether RAIR-like epithelial/thyroid cells are enriched for malignant-like genome-wide expression imbalance.
